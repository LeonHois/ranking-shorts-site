Ranking Shorts Uploader

Ranking Shorts Uploader is a small personal tool. It creates short explainer videos and posts them to the operator's own TikTok and YouTube accounts. It is not a public service: only the operator's own account is connected, and no other users can sign up.

The tool uses the TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API (upload as draft) to send a finished video to the operator's TikTok inbox, where it is reviewed and published manually inside the TikTok app.
